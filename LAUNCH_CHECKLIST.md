# RoktoSheba launch checklist

## Completed in this project

- Bengali-first responsive website with verified RoktoSheba business details.
- Home blood sample collection positioning without claiming laboratory analysis.
- Phone, WhatsApp, and official Facebook links.
- Booking enquiry fallback with required-field validation, Bangladeshi mobile validation, duplicate-submit prevention, and prefilled WhatsApp message.
- FAQ, process, service-area, contact, privacy, and terms sections.
- SEO title, description, keywords, Open Graph metadata, canonical path, LocalBusiness JSON-LD, sitemap, robots file, and route manifest.
- `.gitignore`, `.env.example`, deployment notes, and this checklist.

## Owner verification before launch

- Confirm whether service is available outside Shibbari Mor, Gazipur Sadar, Gazipur.
- Confirm business opening hours and publish them only after verification.
- Confirm applicable collection charges and whether any additional fees apply.
- Confirm which laboratory processes each sample and how reports are delivered.
- Confirm when a doctor’s prescription or laboratory order is required.
- Confirm the final public domain and replace the relative canonical/Open Graph URL if needed.
- Review the generated hero image after completion and approve its final use.
- Connect a secure backend only if persistent booking records, admin notifications, rescheduling, or cancellation are required.
- Perform a final phone test from Bangladesh and review WhatsApp prefilled text on Android and iPhone.

## Not active and intentionally excluded

- No payment processing.
- No report upload or report delivery.
- No patient database.
- No unsupported certifications, reviews, laboratory partnerships, prices, diagnostic guarantees, or exact street address.
- No paid domain, hosting plan, subscription, or analytics service purchased.
